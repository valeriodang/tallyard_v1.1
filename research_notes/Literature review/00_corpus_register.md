# Literature review: corpus register

The folder `reference literature/` (added to `main` in commit 609cf8b on 27 October 2026) holds 84 files. They are 51 text extractions of PDFs, 4 saved web pages and 29 images. Removing three duplicates leaves 52 unique documents. Every file was read in full for this review; the detailed notes are in files 01 to 07 of this folder. Nine more files were added later on 27 October 2026 (D53 to D60, eight documents, one held as both PDF and text); they are reviewed in note 08 and the synthesis is in `reports/Tallyard literature review.md`.

Line references in the notes (L123) point to the file as stored in the folder. Text was extracted from PDFs, so two-column pages and large tables are interleaved; where that garbled a figure the notes say so.

## How to read the columns

- **Type**: peer-reviewed paper, standard, guidance, toolkit, research deliverable, case study, policy paper, report, owner practice, marketing.
- **Authority**: high, medium or low, judged on who wrote it, how it was made and how far it applies to a London reuse platform.
- **Categories**: the 18 knowledge categories in the report, section 2:
  1. Circular economy principles.
  2. Policy and regulation.
  3. Standards and certification.
  4. Structural steel reuse.
  5. Material-specific technical guidance.
  6. Pre-demolition audit and inventory.
  7. Deconstruction and site waste.
  8. Carbon and LCA.
  9. Circularity indicators.
  10. Procurement, contracts and specification.
  11. Business models, supply chain and storage.
  12. Finance, valuation, insurance and warranties.
  13. Owner and developer practice.
  14. Fit-out.
  15. Case studies.
  16. Design for disassembly and design from stock.
  17. Data, digital tools and passports.
  18. Market and economic barriers.
- **Note**: the batch file where the document is reviewed in detail, and any duplicate or extraction issue.

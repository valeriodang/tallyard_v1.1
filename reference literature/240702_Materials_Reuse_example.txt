stone, a great opportunity for circularity through reuse or

Circularity is the degree to which a materials life can 
be / has been extended, reducing waste and limiting 
demand for new replacement materials, both of which are 
associated with negative environmental impact. 

•	 Durable material such as natural stones can last for 
hundreds of years, so are ideal candidates for reuse.

•	 Natural stone has a timeless elegance so can be 
reused in updated designs.

•	 Natural stone is a high value material, so there can be 
financial benefits to reuse or recycling.

Demountability

Potential for reuse greatly depends on ease of recovery

Threaded mechanical fixings are typically the easiest to 
remove and offer the greatest potential for circularity

Nails and other friction based mounting can be removed 
but may damage the material in the process

Adhesive layers are highly variable depending on the 
specific product used during installation, but can greatly 
impede reuse. 

It may be impossible to remove adhesive bound materials 
without breaking them, and some adhesive may remain on 
the material, which limits their reuse options.

Reuse as large slabs Reuse as large slabs is highly sustainable as the materials undergo minimal working and 
are preserved for their future uses. Large slabs can be used as wall or floor cladding, and 
curved slabs can be machined flat or the curve can be integrated into the design. Surface 
refinishing can give the material a new look with minimal wasteage, and can often be 
performed onsite.

Polished Fluted Engraved Engraved and backlit

Remake as smaller tiles Cutting natural stone down to smaller pieces is an irreversible process, however it vastly 
opens up the options and potential for reuse. Smaller pieces can be applied to surfaces 
as tiles, mixed and integrated into various parquet and mosaic styles, and also crafted into  
objects. 

Marble floor at Burlington Arcade Floor in luxury retail  A single slab can create thousands of tiles Broken marble reused in tabletop

Remake as furniture Marble furniture can be an effective way to reuse large stone slabs in a more mobile and 
reconfigurable way. Irregular shapes, quantities, and sizes which cannot be reused on 
floors or walls can be remade as bespoke coffee tables, retail plinths etc. Other materials 
can also be introduced to change the feel of the stone.
